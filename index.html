Data and method

What these numbers count

The data are from the World Bank World Development Indicators API, indicator NY.GDP.PCAP.KD, GDP per capita in constant 2015 US dollars. The World Bank describes this as inflation-adjusted GDP divided by population. The file contains observed country-year records; countries have at least one observation, and have both endpoint values.

One row is one country in one calendar year. The extract includes countries assigned to the World Bank’s Latin America & Caribbean region (LCN), not regional or income-group aggregates. Missing values are not imputed. Countries without a value in either selected endpoint year are excluded from the paired endpoint comparison; for the default 2000–2023 view, countries remain.

Endpoint averages give every matched country equal weight. They are not GDP-weighted regional totals. “Strongest gain” means the largest percentage change in a country’s per-capita value, not the largest dollar increase. Percent change in the headline metric is (2023 equal-country mean ÷ 2000 equal-country mean − 1) × 100. “Countries with an increase” counts matched countries whose 2023 value exceeds their 2000 value. Annual trend points average the available observations in that year among the endpoint-matched countries, so the number of countries contributing to each point can vary slightly.
