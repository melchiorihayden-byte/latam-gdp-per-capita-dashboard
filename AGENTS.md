# Project rules

- Question: Which Latin American and Caribbean countries made the strongest real GDP-per-capita gains from 2000 to 2023?
- Reader: A regional development bank investment strategist.
- Rows counted: One World Bank country-year observation; compare only countries with nonmissing values in both selected endpoint years.
- Period: 2000 through 2023, inclusive.
- Source: World Bank World Development Indicators, indicator NY.GDP.PCAP.KD (GDP per capita, constant 2015 US$), downloaded through the World Bank API.
- Keep all metric definitions consistent across the report, interactive dashboard, and Tableau.
- Keep missing observations missing; do not interpolate. Exclude aggregates and countries without both endpoint observations from endpoint comparisons.
- The country groups are equally weighted in the endpoint averages. Do not pool national GDP or population.
- Recompute every displayed number from data.csv; never type calculated values into the site.
- Do not publish until the data, numbers, and links are reviewed.
